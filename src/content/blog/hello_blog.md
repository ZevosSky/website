# My First Blog Entry

----

Hopefully one of many to come, I plan to use this website as a public journal of sorts. Organized, collected, & opinionated articles on what I'm thinking, doing & making every so often.

My hope is that this becomes a publicly sanitized version of my journal, and that it can be a place where I can share thoughts and opinions, or at worst, a way to trick myself into being accountable.

I often like talking about big-picture topics. Things like economics, philosophy, and psychology.

As a tech person, I understand also that it is very easy to get lost in the nitty gritty details of things, be overly pedantic, and assume everyone has a high level of knowlage about a given topic. I'm going to try and combat my own bias towards this by making every blog article here as layman as possible.
